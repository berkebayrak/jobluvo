What Jobluvo will not do

Invent anything

If a form needs something you have not given, the application waits.

Automate LinkedIn

Against LinkedIn's rules and a risk to your account. Saved LinkedIn jobs are resolved to the employer's own page instead.

Beat bot checks

CAPTCHAs and phone checks are handed to you to complete.
